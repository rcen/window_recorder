🔖 Smart Bookmark Setup

Save interesting pages with title, summary, your notes, and an optional screenshot — all sent to your Window Recorder dashboard.

How to install:

  1. Drag the button below to your browser's Bookmarks Bar.
  2. Visit any interesting page you want to save for later.
  3. Click "📌 Save for Later" in your bookmarks bar.
  4. A popup will appear — add optional notes, tags, and click Save.
  5. The bookmark appears in your activity dashboard above the Stock Market Watch section.
